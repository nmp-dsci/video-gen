Three long films whose tools you can see

Watch these to see what “long” takes on H3: one run by an agent over two weeks, one rendered by a single ComfyUI run, and one made entirely on a home machine.

3 · The craft

Six to watch or read, in this order

All six are about H3. The first three teach the core: planning, references and chaining. The rest add a free pipeline, the hosted route and a written checklist.

Fain: MiniMax H3 AI — Film & TV Production Workflow start here

Story planning, character references, storyboards, prompts, generation and audio. It compares three approaches (shot by shot, one longer scene in one go, and visual storyboards as references) and says what didn't work. The prompts are in a public doc linked from the description.

26 Aug 2026 · 17 min · ComfyUI
Loop Forge: MiniMax H3 reference to video, a short film

A 1-minute film with two characters who stay the same people, in the same restaurant, with the same voices, made with ComfyUI's stock reference-to-video template. The trick: a turnaround sheet, a 3D layout of the room and a voice sample go into the same generation, and the prompt says which subject is in which picture.

29 Aug 2026 · 12 min · RTX 3060 12 GB
Smart Vision: 2 Characters, 2 Voices, 1 Complete AI Film

A 38 s film from two character images and two voice references. The dialogue is one 23 s clip built from four generation windows with Motion Context. The close-up reveal and the ending are separate short clips, so either can be redone without rebuilding the scene.

13 Sep 2026 · 16 min · runs on 8 GB at lower resolution
The AI Brief: can H3 keep multiple characters and their voices consistent?

Several characters and their voices kept consistent across scenes, with a latent upscale at the end. Their free Short Film Director Pipeline goes from Krea stills of the character to a labelled storyboard board, then H3 scene by scene, then plan, review and assemble. No licence is stated, and the weights aren't included.

1 Sep 2026 · 10 min · free ComfyUI workflow on Gumroad
Ai Lockup: I made an entire 3D short film with AI

The hosted route, with no GPU of your own: a screenplay written with ChatGPT, storyboards from GPT Image 2, then H3 on hailuoai.video with consistent characters and voices and lip-sync. The description carries affiliate links.

14 Aug 2026 · 16 min · hosted
Virse: MiniMax H3 storyboard-to-video

A vendor blog, but a sound order of work: build the storyboard, audit it, give each reference one job, group shots by continuity, test at low resolution (“solve direction before fidelity”), then regenerate only the groups that failed. Its memory figures are the author's own tests.

31 Aug 2026 · written · vendor blog

5 · Your 5 minutes

What this means for your film

Time: two data points

BROOD ZERO's 10 minutes took one creator about two weeks. At the Shanghai International Film Festival in June, MiniMax gave four selected creator teams a month each to make an end-to-end AI film on MiniMax Hub, its hosted studio, which has since been renamed MiniMax Design. The lengths of those films aren't stated, and that's the hosted route, not the open weights you're testing.

Render cost: an extrapolation

300 s × 127 min ÷ 177 s
≈ 3.6 h per pass
× 2–3 tries ≈ 7–11 h
× 1.71 units an hour
≈ 12–19 units
≈ $1.20–1.90

That's SeeSee's pace on a 16 GB card at 768×1152. Nobody has measured an L4; runs R1–R4 in s01 will. Paid Colab sessions end after at most 12 hours and take the disk with them, so keep the parts on Drive and plan on more than one session.

Software: ComfyUI changes s01

Nearly every long-form tool here is a ComfyUI node or drives ComfyUI: the Director, CGlide's workflow, The AI Brief's pipeline and infinite-creation. WanGP is still fine for s01's tests, but the 5-minute build points to s01's route C, ComfyUI on Colab. Weigh that in s01's decision D1.

Rights

Use your own characters (AGENTS.md rule 6), and music you made or licensed. The H3 Community Licence has regional limits, which BROOD ZERO's description also warns about, so s00's Q1 still needs an answer.
